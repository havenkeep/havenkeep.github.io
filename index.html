Havenkeep

A document Q&A system that runs on your firm's own machine.

Ask your firm's files a question. Every answer carries its source — one click takes you to the exact passage it came from. If the answer isn't in your files, it says so.

  • Your files stay yours. The system runs on one machine inside your office. Files are indexed, never moved, modified, or deleted. Nothing ever leaves the building.
  • Every answer is verifiable. Page-level citations, version recognition, and an audit log your firm owns.
  • One price, not per seat. A fixed one-time licence per deployment — the whole firm uses it.

Who it's for

Small and mid-sized law firms — 10 to 50 people — whose files live on their own machines or a local document system, and who want their own documents to answer back.

Havenkeep is currently in development. We are speaking with law firms and the IT providers who serve them.
